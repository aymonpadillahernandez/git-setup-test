# Install Lift on your iPhone

About 5 minutes, done once. You do **Part 1** on github.com (computer or phone) and
**Part 2** on your iPhone.

---

## Part 1: Put the app online (one time)

Your iPhone needs a web address to install the app from. GitHub hosts it for free,
but only for public repositories. Making the repository public only shares the app's
code. **Your workouts are never in the repository; they stay on your phone.**

1. Go to **github.com/aymonpadillahernandez/git-setup-test** and tap **Settings**
   (top of the repository page).
2. Scroll to the bottom, to **Danger Zone** → **Change visibility** → **Change to
   public**, and confirm.
3. In the left menu, tap **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - **Source:** *Deploy from a branch*
   - **Branch:** `claude/gym-workout-tracker-app-nk4lys`, folder **/ (root)**
   - Tap **Save**.
5. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the page. At the top it will say
   **"Your site is live at"**:

   **https://aymonpadillahernandez.github.io/git-setup-test/**

> Prefer to keep the repository private? Use Netlify instead (free): go to
> netlify.com → **Sign up with GitHub** → **Add new site** → **Import an existing
> project** → GitHub → pick `git-setup-test` → branch
> `claude/gym-workout-tracker-app-nk4lys` → leave the build settings empty →
> **Deploy**. Netlify gives you an address like `something.netlify.app`; use that
> in Part 2.

---

## Part 2: Install it on your iPhone

1. Open **Safari** (it must be Safari, not Chrome) and go to
   **aymonpadillahernandez.github.io/git-setup-test**
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up). If you don't
   see it, tap **···** first.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **Lift** from your Home Screen.
5. Choose **kg** or **lb**, keep **My 5-day split**, and tap **Get Started**.

That's it. Lift opens full-screen like a normal app and works without signal in
the gym.

---

## Good to know

- **Always open Lift from the Home Screen icon.** The icon and Safari keep separate
  data, so a workout logged in Safari won't appear in the app.
- **Back up once a month:** Today → ⚙︎ (top right) → **Export Backup** → **Save to
  Files**. If you change phones, use **Restore from Backup** on the new one.
- **Updates install themselves.** When the app changes, close Lift and open it
  again (sometimes twice) to get the new version. Your workouts are kept.
- **Change your program anytime** in the **Plan** tab: edit days, exercises, sets
  and rep ranges, or drag to change the order. Rename an exercise in
  ⚙︎ → **Exercises**.
